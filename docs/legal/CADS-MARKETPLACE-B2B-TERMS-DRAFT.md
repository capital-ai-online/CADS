# CADS Marketplace B2B Terms — Draft

Status: **DRAFT — OWNER / LEGAL APPROVAL REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLICATION**

These draft terms describe the intended commercial boundary for CADS sold as a B2B subscription
through GitHub Marketplace. They are not final legal terms.

## Service

CADS provides evidence-bound software/architecture benchmark and governance functionality according
to the purchased plan. Feature availability is determined by the authoritative Marketplace
entitlement and the versioned CADS capability contract.

## Subscription and billing

Billing is handled by GitHub Marketplace under the Marketplace plan selected by the customer.
Starter, Pro and Enterprise are intended as paid plans with monthly and annual billing.

No repository document may invent or override the actual Marketplace USD price or plan ID.

## License / access right

During an active subscription, the customer receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use
the hosted CADS service and installed GitHub App functionality for the subscribed account, subject
to the applicable plan limits and these final terms once approved.

No source-code ownership or trademark right is transferred.

## Security and evidence boundary

CADS scores, benchmark results and reports are evidence for engineering decisions. They do not by
themselves constitute:

- a security certification;
- legal or license approval;
- regulatory certification;
- production release authorization.

## Customer responsibilities

The customer remains responsible for:

- authorization to connect the selected repositories;
- lawful processing of its repository/customer data;
- protecting its GitHub credentials and organization access;
- reviewing CADS evidence in the context of its own release/governance process.

## Suspension / cancellation

Access follows the authoritative GitHub Marketplace subscription state. On effective cancellation,
CADS deactivates the Marketplace entitlement and removes applicable customer data within the
documented retention/deletion window.

## Third-party services

GitHub, Supabase and other explicitly disclosed infrastructure remain third-party services subject to
their own terms.

## Finalization gate

Before publication this draft requires review of, at minimum:

- contracting entity;
- governing law / venue;
- warranty and liability limitations;
- service availability/SLA language;
- data-processing/privacy terms;
- tax/VAT treatment where relevant;
- support and termination terms;
- GitHub Marketplace Developer Agreement compatibility.
